# Shield-Insurance-Analysis-Report
---
## 🧩 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing business performance for Shield Insurance, a growing insurance provider.
The dashboard provides actionable insights into revenue trends, customer growth, policy performance, and sales channels to support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis was built using Power BI, transforming raw data into interactive and meaningful business insights.

--- 

## 🎯 Objective

Analyze customer growth and revenue trends

Understand sales performance across different channels

Identify high-performing segments (age group, policy type)

Track daily and monthly business performance

Support stakeholders with data-driven insights

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI

Power Query (Data Transformation)

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

Excel (Data Source)

--- 

## 📊 Dashboard Features

**1️⃣ Executive Overview**

    Total Revenue

    Total Customers

    Daily Growth Trends

    KPI Cards for quick insights

**2️⃣ Sales Mode Analysis**

    Performance by sales channel

    Online vs Offline contribution
  
    Channel-wise growth trends

**3️⃣ Age Group Analysis**

    Customer segmentation by age

    Revenue contribution by age group

    Behavioral patterns

---

## 📌 Key Insights

    Certain age groups contribute significantly higher revenue
    
    Sales mode impacts customer acquisition trends
    
    Revenue shows consistent growth patterns over time
    
    Customer base expansion highlights business scalability opportunities

## 📈 Business Impact

This dashboard helps stakeholders:

    Identify growth opportunities
    
    Optimize sales strategies
    
    Improve customer targeting
    
    Monitor business performance in real-time
